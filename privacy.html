Privacy Policy

APK Pocket: APK Extractor · Package dev.apkpocket.app
Effective date: 12 October 2026

Summary: installed-app inventory, favorites, settings and export history are handled on-device. APK Pocket does not upload installed-app inventory or exported APK/ZIP files to the publisher. The current Play release contains no advertising SDK, billing SDK or analytics. External network activity occurs only when the user chooses to open this policy in a browser or send feedback through an email application.

1. Installed-app information and exports

APK Pocket reads information Android makes available about installed apps, including app/package name, icon, version, system/update classification, APK size and readable installed APK paths. In the full-visibility edition, broad package visibility is used only for the core on-device app-discovery and APK-extraction workflow.

Installed-app inventory is processed on the device. APK Pocket does not sell installed-app inventory, use it for advertising targeting, or send the inventory to the purchase-verification service or advertising SDK.

APK/ZIP exports are created from installed package files that Android allows APK Pocket to read. Exports exclude private app data, passwords, accounts and sign-in sessions. Files are saved to a destination the user chooses or shared through Android. APK Pocket does not upload exported APK/ZIP files to its own backend.

2. Local app data

Settings, favorites and export history are stored in APK Pocket's app-private storage. Android backup is disabled. Local app data can be cleared through Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Files the user exported separately are not removed automatically when app data is cleared.

3. Feedback by email

When the user chooses Feedback, APK Pocket opens a compatible email application using Android's email-intent flow. The recipient is pdnexus.apps@gmail.com and the subject is App Feedback. APK Pocket pre-fills the app version, device model and Android version, plus a blank area for the user's message.

APK Pocket does not automatically send the email and does not send feedback through an APK Pocket feedback server or database. The user can review or edit the draft and decides whether to tap Send in the chosen email application. If the user sends it, the user's email provider and the receiving email service process the message under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Advertising and purchases

The current Play release of APK Pocket contains no advertising, advertising SDK, User Messaging Platform SDK, billing SDK or Remove Ads purchase. If a future release introduces advertising or purchases, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before that release.

6. Network security and permissions

The app does not request GPS/location permission, contacts permission, broad storage permission, Internet permission or permission to install apps. The full-visibility edition requests Android's broad package-visibility permission because discovering arbitrary installed apps is part of its core extraction purpose.

7. Children

APK Pocket is a general Android utility and is not designed specifically for children. The publisher must keep the Play Store target-audience declaration consistent with final marketing and distribution choices.

8. Third-party services

APK Pocket is distributed through Google Play and can open the user's chosen browser and email application. Google Play, the browser provider and the email provider may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local settings, favorites, export history and temporary files remain on the device until the app's cleanup behavior, in-app clearing actions, uninstallation or Android app-data controls remove them. Email feedback is retained according to the sender's and recipient's email-service settings.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when APK Pocket's features, providers or legal requirements change. The same public HTTPS policy URL should remain linked from Google Play and Settings in the app.

For support or privacy requests, contact pdnexus.apps@gmail.com.
